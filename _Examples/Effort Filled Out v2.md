# Project: Learn Spanish Basics

## Objective & Goal

**What**: Achieve basic conversational Spanish (A2 level) by end of September 2026.

**Why**: Expanding language skills for travel and professional networking; personal interest in Romance languages.

**Success looks like**:
- Can introduce myself and hold a 5-minute conversation on familiar topics
- Understand most spoken Spanish in slow, clear speech
- Read simple texts like emails and basic articles
- Conjugate present tense verbs consistently

## Scope & Requirements

**Must Have**:
- Complete Duolingo Spanish course units 1–8
- Learn 500+ vocabulary words (organized by topic)
- Understand present, past, and future tense basics
- Practice speaking via language exchange or tutoring

**Nice to Have**:
- Visit a Spanish-speaking country and test live conversation
- Read a short story in Spanish
- Watch Spanish TV shows with subtitles
