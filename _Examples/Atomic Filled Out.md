## 🧠 One-liner

Small, consistent gains compound over time — a principle that applies to habits, knowledge, finances, and personal growth equally, turning marginal improvements into transformative results.

## 🧩 Notes & Sketches

Compound interest is often presented as a purely financial concept, but its power lies deeper. The same mathematics apply to:
- **Learning**: Reading 10 pages daily for a year accumulates into 3,650 pages of knowledge
- **Habits**: 1% daily improvement = 37x growth in a year (Atomic Habits principle)
- **Relationships**: Small consistent actions deepen trust over time
- **Energy**: Small recoveries compounded prevent burnout

The key insight is the exponential curve — there's a long flat section where nothing seems to happen, then a steep rise. Most people quit before the curve lifts.

Inverse: compounding works against you too. 1% daily decline also compounds. Procrastination, technical debt, and resentment all follow this pattern.
