# 📋 Things Folder Contract

**What**: Specific, discrete entities worth documenting — physical objects, phenomena, artifacts, named things with properties or significance. One thing per note with description, characteristics, and context.

**When**: Created when you want to track or reference a specific thing beyond passing mention. Updated as you learn more about it.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/Atomics/Things/` — one `.md` file per entity or artifact.

**Lifecycle**: Create when you encounter something worth documenting. Link from related concepts, areas, and tools. Archive if the thing becomes obsolete or no longer relevant.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Specific objects, artifacts, or products you reference repeatedly
- Named phenomena or entities worth documenting
- Things you want to track properties, history, or significance of
- Specific resources, locations, or artifacts meaningful to your work

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Abstract concepts or patterns** → [[Concepts]] (how things work)
- **Tool-specific documentation** → [[Tools]] (software/tools you actively use)
- **General ideas** → [[Ideas]] (abstract thoughts)
- **Source material** → [[04-Sources]] (external origin)
