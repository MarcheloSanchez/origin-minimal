# 📋 Atomics Folder Contract

**What**: Timeless, declarative knowledge you author—one idea per note. Concepts (how things work), Ideas (generative), Things (entities), Statements (claims), Quotes (captured passages). Not tied to projects or external sources.

**When**: Daily capture; weekly linking review; monthly connection audit; quarterly promotion to MOCs.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/Atomics/` — Concepts, Ideas, Things, Statements, Quotes (each in its own subfolder for organization).

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd Atomic choice (→ `Templates/Static/atomic.md`). Promote by setting `maturity: 🌲evergreen` and adding to an MOC when referenced repeatedly or guides decisions. Archive if obsoleted or merged.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- **Concepts**: Mental models, principles, frameworks (how something works)
- **Ideas**: Generative thoughts, insights, original observations
- **Things**: Objects, entities, tools, resources with documentation
- **Statements**: Claims, assertions, beliefs with supporting context
- **Quotes**: Captured passages or wisdom from sources (with attribution + link to source note)

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Source material (articles, books, papers)** → [[04-Sources]] (external origin, needs processing)
- **Project work and goals** → [[03-Efforts]] (time-bounded, actionable, has an end)
- **Areas (ongoing life domains)** → [[Areas]] (ongoing, not atomic ideas)
- **Navigation/curation** → [[01-MOCs]] (organizes without adding new knowledge)

## 📖 Real Atomic Examples

> [!example]+ **Concept: Systems Thinking**
> - **Idea**: Complex problems often result from feedback loops and delayed effects, not single causes
> - **Key Insight**: Changing one variable creates ripple effects across the system that emerge over time
> - **Application**: Used in team workflow redesign (`[[Effort: Team Process Optimization]]`), debugging complex bugs
> - **Related Concepts**: `[[Leverage Points]]`, `[[Mental Models]]`, `[[Causal Loop Diagrams]]`
> - **Sources**: `[[Source: Thinking in Systems — Meadows]]`

> [!example]+ **Idea: Learning Through Teaching**
> - **Hypothesis**: Explaining concepts to others deepens personal understanding and reveals knowledge gaps
> - **Evidence**: Feynman Technique effectiveness, mentoring success patterns
> - **Practice**: Use when learning new domain — plan to teach someone within 2 weeks
> - **Related**: `[[Effort: Mentor Junior Developer]]`, `[[Active Recall]]`

> [!example]+ **Statement: Knowledge Decay**
> - **Claim**: Without practice and retrieval, knowledge fades predictably over time (forgetting curve)
> - **Implication**: Spaced repetition and application beats one-time learning
> - **Application Pattern**: Regular review schedules, project-based application, teaching cycles
> - **Source Attribution**: `[[Source: Make It Stick — Brown, Roediger, McDaniel]]`
