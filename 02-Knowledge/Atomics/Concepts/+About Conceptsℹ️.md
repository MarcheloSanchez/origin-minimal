# 📋 Concepts Folder Contract

**What**: Named patterns, theories, and mental models — explanations of how things work in the world. One concept per note with definition, key insights, and related concepts.

**When**: Created when you encounter a named pattern worth understanding and referencing. Reviewed when applying to new contexts.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/Atomics/Concepts/` — one `.md` file per named concept or framework.

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd Atomic choice (→ Concepts folder). Link from Ideas and Things that use the concept. Archive when concept becomes superseded or irrelevant.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Named concepts, theories, frameworks (e.g., Systems Thinking, Spaced Repetition)
- Mental models and heuristics with established definitions
- Principles and patterns that explain how things work
- Documented mechanisms and processes with academic or professional grounding

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Your own personal insights** → [[Ideas]] (emerging, not established)
- **Physical things or tools** → [[Things]] (objects, not patterns)
- **Original ideas you invented** → [[Ideas]] (even if concept-like)
- **Source material** → [[04-Sources]] (needs processing first)

## 📖 Real Concept Examples

> [!example]+ **Concept: Spaced Repetition**
> - **Definition**: Reviewing information at increasing intervals increases long-term retention compared to massed practice
> - **Key Mechanism**: Each successful retrieval extends the interval before the next review; failure resets it shorter
> - **Applications**: Flashcard systems, learning schedules, skill practice routines
> - **Source**: Cognitive psychology research; implemented in tools like Anki
> - **Related Concepts**: `[[Active Recall]]`, `[[Forgetting Curve]]`, `[[Deliberate Practice]]`

> [!example]+ **Concept: Pareto Principle (80/20)**
> - **Definition**: Roughly 80% of results come from 20% of effort in many domains
> - **Implication**: Focus effort on the high-leverage 20% for maximum return
> - **Applications**: Task prioritization, resource allocation, quality focus
> - **Domains**: Business, personal productivity, decision-making
> - **Related Concepts**: `[[Leverage Points]]`, `[[Opportunity Cost]]`, `[[Systems Thinking]]`
