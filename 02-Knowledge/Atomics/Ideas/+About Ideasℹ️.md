# 📋 Ideas Folder Contract

**What**: Your original, emerging insights and personal observations — provisional thoughts authored by you before they mature into established concepts. One idea per note capturing a generative observation.

**When**: Created when you have a personal insight worth capturing and developing. Reviewed when experiences test or refine the idea.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/Atomics/Ideas/` — one `.md` file per personal insight or observation.

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd Atomic choice (→ Ideas folder). Develop by collecting evidence, examples, and related insights. Can mature into Concepts if they become established patterns worth naming broadly.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Original personal insights and observations
- Emerging opinions and perspectives you're exploring
- Half-formed thoughts worth developing further
- Hypotheses about how things work (before testing)
- Interpretations and frameworks you're building

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Established concepts or theories** → [[Concepts]] (named, defined, grounded)
- **Source highlights and quotes** → [[04-Sources]] (external origin, needs attribution)
- **Project work and goals** → [[03-Efforts]] (actionable, time-bounded)
- **Fleeting thoughts** → [[+Inbox]] (capture first, develop if valuable)

## 📖 Real Idea Examples

> [!example]+ **Idea: Knowledge Compounds Like Interest**
> - **Observation**: Small regular learning creates exponential growth over years through compounding connections
> - **Personal Application**: 15-min daily learning beats occasional intensive study
> - **Testing**: Tracking skill progression over quarters and years
> - **Related Concepts**: `[[Spaced Repetition]]`, `[[Compound Growth]]`, `[[Systems Thinking]]`

> [!example]+ **Idea: Decision Quality Improves with Constraints**
> - **Hypothesis**: Clear constraints (time, budget, scope) often lead to better decisions than unlimited options
> - **Observation**: Best projects happen with focused parameters, worst with unlimited freedom
> - **Applications**: Use in effort planning, priority setting
> - **Related Concepts**: `[[Pareto Principle]]`, `[[Opportunity Cost]]`
