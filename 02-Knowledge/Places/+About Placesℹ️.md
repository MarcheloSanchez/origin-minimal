# 📋 Places Folder Contract

**What**: Knowledge and context about specific locations—geography, culture, practical intelligence, personal meaning, and recommendations. One place per note capturing your experience and understanding.

**When**: Created when visiting a place worth remembering, or during trip planning. Updated with new discoveries and deeper understanding.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/Places/` — one `.md` file per location, organized by region or type as needed.

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd Place choice (→ `Templates/Static/place.md`). Link from related experiences, people, efforts. Archive if location becomes inaccessible or no longer relevant.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Specific locations (cities, neighborhoods, venues, workplaces) worth documenting
- Geographic knowledge, culture, and practical intelligence
- Personal experiences and meaningful memories attached to places
- Recommendations, tips, and discoveries
- Connections to people and activities in that location

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Time-anchored travel logs** → [[05-Calendar]] (daily/weekly notes, journal entries)
- **Calendar events** → [[05-Calendar]] (trip dates, scheduled visits)
- **General travel advice** → [[Atomics]] (concepts, not specific places)
- **Meeting notes at location** → [[Meetings]] (meeting records)
