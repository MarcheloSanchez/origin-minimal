# 📋 Knowledge Folder Contract

**What**: The thinking layer—timeless knowledge units (Concepts, Ideas, Things, Statements, Quotes) and ongoing life domains (Areas). One idea per note; each note links outward to related knowledge and inward from source material.

**When**: Daily capture and linking; weekly connection review; monthly curation; quarterly architecture review.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/` — Atomics (Concepts, Ideas, Things, Statements, Quotes), Areas (Health, Finance, Career, Relationships, Personal), People, Places, Tools, plus `X` (overflow).

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd choice (Atomic → `Templates/Static/atomic.md`; Area → `Templates/Static/area.md`). Archive by setting `status: 📦archived`, then move to `06-Archive/` when knowledge becomes obsolete or superseded.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- **Atomics (one idea each)**: Concepts (how something works), Ideas (generative), Things (entities/objects), Statements (claims), Quotes (captured passages)
- **Areas (5 life domains)**: Health, Finance, Career, Relationships, Personal — ongoing domains with Purpose, Metrics, Active Efforts
- **People**: Person notes with context, relationships, expertise
- **Places**: Location notes with characteristics, significance
- **Tools**: Tools/resources/software with documentation and use cases

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Projects/time-bounded work** → [[03-Efforts|Efforts]] (has an end date, actionable)
- **External sources (articles, books, media)** → [[04-Sources|Sources]] (external origin, needs processing)
- **Navigation/curation-only notes** → [[01-MOCs|MOCs]] (organizes but doesn't contain knowledge)
- **Workflow tutorials** → [[🔁My PKM Workflows]] (how to use the system, not knowledge itself)

## 📖 Real Knowledge Examples

> [!example]+ **💡 Concept: Spaced Repetition**
> - **What**: Cognitive principle — spacing practice over time increases long-term retention
> - **Why Important**: Foundation for effective learning and skill maintenance
> - **Key Points**: Optimal intervals increase after each successful recall; better than massed practice
> - **Related**: `[[Active Recall]]`, `[[Memory Consolidation]]`, `[[Effort: Learning Spanish]]`
> - **Application**: Used in daily review practices, flashcard systems, training programs

> [!example]+ **🏠 Area: Career Development**
> - **Purpose**: Advance professional capabilities and create meaningful work impact
> - **Current Status**: 🟢 Thriving
> - **Key Metrics**: New skills quarterly, professional network growth, project delivery
> - **Active Efforts**: `[[Effort: ISTQB Certification]]`, `[[Effort: Leadership Skills]]`
> - **Next Focus**: Data science transition, technical leadership skill building
