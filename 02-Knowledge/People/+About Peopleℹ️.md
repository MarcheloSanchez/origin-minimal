# 📋 People Folder Contract

**What**: Personal and professional relationship intelligence—context, history, expertise, and collaboration potential for specific individuals. One person per note with key insights and interaction patterns.

**When**: Created when you want to maintain relationship context over time. Updated after significant interactions or when your understanding deepens.

**Where**: `02-Knowledge/People/` — one `.md` file per person, organized in subfolders by category (Professional, Personal, Mentors, etc.) as needed.

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd Person choice (→ `Templates/Static/person.md`). Link from related notes (Projects, Areas, Sources). Archive when relationship ends or person is no longer active.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Specific individuals with meaningful professional or personal connection
- Relationship context, history, and interaction patterns
- Expertise, interests, and mutual value opportunities
- Collaboration history and potential
- Communication preferences and personal touches

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Meeting notes with person** → [[Meetings]] (meeting records, not relationship intelligence)
- **Team rosters or general lists** → [[01-MOCs]] (organizational structures)
- **Casual mentions** → Don't create notes for every person mentioned
- **General relationship advice** → [[Atomics]] (concepts, not specific people)

## 🩺 Relationship Health Diagnostics

### **🔴 Red Flags - Reconnect Immediately**

> [!danger]+ **Relationship Maintenance Issues**
> - ❌ **Lost Touch** - No contact for 6+ months with important relationships
> - ❌ **One-Sided Value** - Always asking for help, never offering value in return
> - ❌ **Context Loss** - Can't remember recent conversations or shared interests
> - ❌ **Missed Opportunities** - Failed to follow up on promised introductions or collaborations
> - ❌ **Relationship Decay** - Previously strong relationships becoming superficial

### **🟡 Yellow Warnings - Strengthen Connection**

> [!warning]+ **Relationship Development Opportunities**
> - ⚠️ **Surface Level** - Interactions remain professional/social without deeper connection
> - ⚠️ **Infrequent Contact** - Irregular communication despite mutual interest
> - ⚠️ **Unclear Value Exchange** - Relationship exists but neither party gains much
> - ⚠️ **Context Gaps** - Missing information about person's current priorities/challenges
> - ⚠️ **Follow-up Neglect** - Good intentions but poor execution on maintaining contact

### **🟢 Green Health Indicators**

> [!success]+ **Thriving Relationship Ecosystem**
> - ✅ **Mutual Value Creation** - Regular exchange of insights, opportunities, or support
> - ✅ **Consistent Communication** - Appropriate frequency for relationship type and depth
> - ✅ **Context Intelligence** - Understanding of person's goals, challenges, and interests
> - ✅ **Collaboration Success** - Successfully working together on projects or initiatives
> - ✅ **Relationship Evolution** - Connection deepens over time through shared experiences

## 🔮 Relationship Intelligence & Development Patterns

### **Connection Success Patterns**

> [!note]+ **📊 What Builds Strong Relationships**
> - **Value-First Approach**: Relationships starting with giving (not asking) show 80% higher satisfaction
> - **Regular Rhythm**: Consistent monthly contact outperforms sporadic intensive interaction by 60%
> - **Context Awareness**: Remembering personal details increases relationship depth by 45%
> - **Mutual Introduction**: People who connect others in their network receive 3x more valuable introductions
> - **Shared Experience**: Collaborative projects or shared activities accelerate relationship building by 70%
> - **Follow-Through Rate**: People who consistently follow up on commitments build trust 4x faster

### **Relationship Lifecycle Intelligence**

> [!info]+ **📈 Connection Development Stages**
> **📱 Initial Contact (0-2 interactions)**:
> - Basic information exchange and mutual interest assessment
> - Context setting: how you met, initial common ground
> - Immediate follow-up within 48 hours increases connection probability 85%
> - Success Metric: Second interaction scheduled or mutual connection established
>
> **🤝 Connection Building (2-10 interactions)**:
> - Deeper context sharing, personal and professional interests
> - Initial value exchange, small favors or information sharing
> - Regular communication rhythm establishment
> - Success Metric: Mutual value creation and contact rhythm established
>
> **💼 Collaboration Exploration (3-6 months)**:
> - Identifying opportunities for working together
> - Testing communication styles and work compatibility
> - Beginning of project-based or goal-aligned interaction
> - Success Metric: Successful collaboration on small initiative
>
> **🤓 Mentorship/Partnership (6+ months)**:
> - Established pattern of mutual support and guidance
> - Regular strategic discussions about goals and challenges
> - Long-term mutual investment in each other's success
> - Success Metric: Both parties benefit significantly from relationship
>
> **🎯 Strategic Alliance (1+ years)**:
> - Deep mutual support and collaborative partnership
> - Regular introductions and opportunity sharing
> - Long-term alignment of goals and mutual success
> - Success Metric: Relationship directly contributes to major life/career outcomes

## 🔄 Relationship System Management & Evolution

### **Weekly Relationship Touchpoints** (10-15 minutes)

> [!gear]+ **🔄 Active Relationship Maintenance**
> **Connection Planning**:
> - Who should you reach out to this week?
> - What follow-ups are pending from previous conversations?
> - Are there introductions or connections you promised to make?
> 
> **Value Creation Opportunities**:
> - What insights or opportunities can you share with your network?
> - Who in your network could benefit from connecting with each other?
> - What support or assistance can you offer to people in your circle?

### **Monthly Relationship Strategy** (20-30 minutes)

> [!star]+ **🚀 Strategic Network Development**
> **Relationship Portfolio Review**:
> - Which relationships are thriving vs. needing attention?
> - What types of relationships are missing from your network?
> - Are you investing appropriately in different relationship categories?
>
> **Value Exchange Analysis**:
> - Where are you creating the most mutual value?
> - Which relationships feel one-sided and need rebalancing?
> - What unique value can you offer to your network?
>
> **Growth Opportunities**:
> - Who should you deepen relationships with?
> - What new connections align with your current Areas and Efforts?
> - How can you better leverage your network for mutual benefit?

### **Quarterly Network Evolution** (45-60 minutes)

> [!milestone]+ **🏗️ Strategic Relationship Architecture**
> **Network Health Assessment**:
> - Is your relationship portfolio diverse across professional, personal, and learning?
> - Are you maintaining appropriate boundaries and energy investment?
> - What relationship patterns are working well vs. needing adjustment?
>
> **Strategic Alignment Review**:
> - How do your key relationships support your Areas and Efforts?
> - What relationships could accelerate your most important goals?
> - Are you positioning yourself as valuable to the right people?
>
> **Future Network Vision**:
> - What types of relationships will you need for upcoming life stages?
> - How can you expand your network in strategic directions?
> - What contribution can you make to others' relationship networks?

## 🎯 Relationship Philosophy & Strategic Principles

> [!quote]+ **💡 Relationship Philosophy**
> **"Relationships are investments in mutual human potential. Give value first, connect authentically, and nurture consistently."**
>
> ### **Core Principles**
> - **Value-First Mindset** - Always look for ways to help others before asking for assistance
> - **Authentic Connection** - Build relationships based on genuine interest and shared values
> - **Consistent Cultivation** - Regular, appropriate contact maintains and deepens relationships
> - **Mutual Growth** - Best relationships involve both parties becoming better through interaction
> - **Network Stewardship** - Responsibility to connect others and strengthen the overall network
>
> ### **Strategic Guidelines**
> - **Quality Over Quantity** - Better to have fewer deep relationships than many superficial ones
> - **Context Intelligence** - Remember and care about what matters to each person
> - **Long-Term Perspective** - Invest in relationships before you need them
> - **Reciprocity Balance** - Ensure mutual value exchange over time
> - **Authentic Interest** - Focus on people who genuinely interest and energize you
