> **07-Prompts** — Operational/procedural. Instructions and recipes you (and the AI) *operate from*: Prompts you give to AI, Playbooks you follow as procedures. You run these, you don't just read them. *Belongs here if:* it's an instruction you give an AI or a step-by-step procedure you execute. *Does NOT belong:* guides someone else wrote that you saved (→ 04-Sources/Guides); knowledge about prompting theory (→ 02-Knowledge/Atomics).

# About Prompts

Prompt library. The rule here is **rate, don't document**. A prompt only needs a good title, one category, and a status you set the *first time you actually use it*. Nothing else is mandatory.
