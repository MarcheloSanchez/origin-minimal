# 📋 Books Folder Contract

**What**: Long-form published works—non-fiction, fiction, textbooks (150+ pages or 5+ hours reading). One book per note with notes, key insights, and extracted knowledge.

**When**: 1 book per 2-4 weeks (deep processing requires time). Progressive note-taking during reading; processing 2-4 weeks after completion.

**Where**: `04-Sources/Books/` — one `.md` file per book.

**Lifecycle**: Create when starting book. Process progressively while reading. Extract key insights and link to knowledge/efforts. Archive when finished and value extracted.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Non-fiction books (business, science, biography, self-help)
- Fiction (novels, short stories for inspiration/themes)
- Textbooks and comprehensive guides (150+ pages)
- Memoirs and autobiographies

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Short articles and blog posts** → [[Articles]] (quick reads)
- **Structured courses** → [[Courses]] (video-based, sequential)
- **Academic papers** → [[Research]] (rigorous methodology)
- **Your own writing** → [[02-Knowledge]] or [[07-Prompts]]
