# 📋 Meetings Folder Contract

**What**: Meeting notes—what was discussed, decisions made, action items. External context from meetings, calls, discussions.

**When**: Captured during or immediately after. Processed within 1 day for actions. Archived when follow-ups complete.

**Where**: `04-Sources/Meetings/` — one `.md` file per meeting.

**Lifecycle**: Create during meeting. Extract decisions and actions. Link to efforts. Archive when done.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Meeting notes (who, what, key decisions)
- Action items and follow-ups
- Workshop and training notes
- Discussion summaries

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Calendar events** → [[05-Calendar]] (scheduling)
- **Your own decisions** → [[02-Knowledge]] (your thinking)
- **Work logs** → [[05-Calendar]] (time-bound logs)
- **Person notes** → [[People]] (relationship context)
