# 📋 Articles Folder Contract

**What**: Short-form external writing—blog posts, essays, opinion pieces, newsletters (10-60 min reads). One article per note with URL, key insights, and connections.

**When**: Batch processed weekly (5-7 articles sustainable). Quick to capture (1 min), fast to process (15-30 min).

**Where**: `04-Sources/Articles/` — one `.md` file per article.

**Lifecycle**: Create when capturing. Process within 1-2 weeks. Extract key insights and link to relevant knowledge/efforts. Archive if no longer relevant.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Blog posts and essays (500-3000 words)
- Opinion pieces and commentary
- News articles and trend pieces
- Newsletter articles and excerpts
- Short-form guides written by others (with personal context)

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Long-form books and textbooks** → [[Books]] (deep, sustained reading)
- **Structured courses** → [[Courses]] (sequential learning, formal instruction)
- **Academic papers** → [[Research]] (rigorous methodology, citations)
- **Your own thoughts or procedures** → [[02-Knowledge]] or [[07-Prompts]]
