# 📋 Courses Folder Contract

**What**: Structured learning sequences—online courses, video playlists, curricula (Udemy, Coursera, YouTube series). Multiple lessons building progressively toward skill mastery.

**When**: Lesson-by-lesson over weeks (2-3 courses sustainable). Practice exercises during learning; consolidation after completion.

**Where**: `04-Sources/Courses/` — one `.md` file per course.

**Lifecycle**: Create when enrolling. Process actively during lessons. Extract key skills and link to efforts. Mark complete when finished and skills applied.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Online courses (Udemy, Coursera, Skillshare, LinkedIn Learning)
- YouTube educational playlists and video series
- Structured workshop series with multiple sessions
- Formal curricula and bootcamp materials
- Any multi-lesson learning sequence

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Single articles or videos** → [[Articles]] or [[Media]]
- **Long-form books** → [[Books]] (self-paced reading)
- **Academic papers** → [[Research]] (methodology-focused)
- **Your own implementations** → [[02-Knowledge]] or [[07-Prompts]]
