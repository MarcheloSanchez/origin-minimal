# 📋 Sources Folder Contract

**What**: External knowledge inputs—books, articles, videos, courses, meetings, research. Material authored by others that you capture, process, extract insights from, and connect to your knowledge.

**When**: Capture during consumption; process within 1-2 weeks; reference ongoing as part of knowledge network.

**Where**: `04-Sources/` — Books, Articles, Courses, Guides, Media, Meetings, Research (subfolders by type).

**Lifecycle**: Create when capturing external material. Mark processed when key insights extracted and connected to Knowledge. Archive or delete if no longer relevant.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Books, articles, papers, research (with your processing and insights)
- Courses, videos, podcasts (with notes and learnings)
- Meeting notes (what was discussed, decisions made, action items)
- Guides and how-to material (with your context and applications)
- Media and presentations (with extracted value)

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Your own insights distilled from sources** → [[02-Knowledge]] (becomes your atomic knowledge)
- **Procedures you authored** → [[07-Prompts]] (your playbooks, not external material)
- **Time-anchored meeting logs** → [[05-Calendar]] (calendar events and daily records)
- **Bookmark collections** → Delete or archive (not processed knowledge)
