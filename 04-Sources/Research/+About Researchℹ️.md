# 📋 Research Folder Contract

**What**: Peer-reviewed and rigorous sources—academic papers, studies, reports, whitepapers. Empirically grounded with methodology and citations.

**When**: Scanned for relevance (5 min). Deep read for methodology and findings (30-90 min). Processed within 2 weeks.

**Where**: `04-Sources/Research/` — one `.md` file per paper/study.

**Lifecycle**: Create when capturing. Extract methodology and key findings. Note limitations. Link to concepts. Cite in related work.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Peer-reviewed journal articles and papers
- Published research studies and meta-analyses
- Technical reports and whitepapers
- Case studies with methodology
- Academic theses and dissertations

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Opinion pieces** → [[Articles]] (commentary)
- **Comprehensive books** → [[Books]] (narrative-based)
- **Online courses** → [[Courses]] (instruction)
- **Your own research** → [[02-Knowledge]] (your findings)

## 📖 Real Research Examples

> [!example]+ **Paper: Spaced Repetition in Learning**
> - **Authors**: Cepeda, Pashler, et al. (2006)
> - **Finding**: Spacing intervals of 10-20x retention interval optimal
> - **Application**: Informed study scheduling and flashcard systems
> - **Citation**: Used in `[[Learning Theory]]` notes

> [!example]+ **Study: Digital Distraction and Productivity**
> - **Focus**: Effects of notifications on focus
> - **Finding**: Frequent notifications reduce focus 40%
> - **Application**: Shaped notification settings, deep work practices

## 📖 Research Characteristics

**Strengths:**
- **Rigorous evidence** — Empirically grounded, methodology transparent
- **Citation authority** — Can cite in your own work and projects
- **Deep methodology** — Understand *how* conclusions were reached
- **Peer-reviewed** — Subject to expert scrutiny; reliable insights
- **Trend identification** — Research clusters reveal emerging patterns in a field

**Pitfalls:**
- **High density** — Takes longer to extract value than other source types
- **Jargon and complexity** — Academic language requires sustained focus
- **Limited immediate application** — Research rarely offers "do this tomorrow" steps
- **Over-citation** — Risk of over-quoting without synthesis or personal interpretation

---

## 🔄 Processing Flow

1. **Initial Scan** (5 min): Read title, abstract, conclusion; assess relevance and rigor
2. **Deep Read** (30-90 min): Understand methodology, key findings, limitations
3. **Extract Findings** (10 min): Summarize methodology, results, and implications
4. **Connect** (10 min): Link to existing knowledge (Areas, Efforts, or related research)
5. **Citation Capture** (5 min): Record full citation, DOI, and key quotes for referencing
6. **Archive**: Store for future citation or methodological reference
