# 📋 Calendar Folder Contract

**What**: Time-anchored notes—daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly reviews and reflections. Presence implies a specific date.

**When**: Daily capture. Weekly reflections. Quarterly/yearly strategy planning.

**Where**: `05-Calendar/` → Daily/, Weekly/, Monthly/, Quarterly/, Yearly/ subfolders.

**Lifecycle**: Create on schedule. Review and reflect. Archive old notes. Extract insights to Knowledge.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Daily notes (experiences, reflections, captures)
- Weekly reviews (patterns, progress, learning)
- Monthly reflections (strategy, progress assessment)
- Quarterly and yearly reviews (planning, evolution)

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Evergreen knowledge** → [[02-Knowledge]] (timeless)
- **Projects/efforts** → [[03-Efforts]] (time-independent)
- **External sources** → [[04-Sources]] (to be processed)
