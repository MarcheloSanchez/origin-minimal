## 🗺️ Overview
Node Praha is a mid-size coworking space in Prague 2, used as a primary deep-work location outside the home office. Chosen for its quiet zones, stable Wi-Fi, and proximity to the metro. Serves as an intentional context switch — going here signals "focus mode" to the brain, which makes it useful for high-concentration creative work.
