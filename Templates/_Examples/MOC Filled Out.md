# MOC – Personal Knowledge Management

## Abstract
Personal Knowledge Management encompasses systems, tools, and practices for capturing, organizing, connecting, and retrieving information to support learning and decision-making. This domain covers digital note-taking methods, information architecture, workflow design, and the intersection of technology with cognitive processes. Unlike simple note-taking, PKM focuses on creating an external thinking system that amplifies personal intelligence.
