## 💬 Summary
Adam Novák is a Prague-based product leader and long-time PKM practitioner. Met him at a local productivity meetup in late 2024. He runs his own Notion setup that closely mirrors Origin's PARA logic, which made our exchange immediately practical. He's been a consistent sounding board for the vault's design decisions.

---

## 🧩 Context & Relationship
- **First contact / discovery:** ProductiveCZE meetup, November 2024 — introduced by a mutual contact in the Obsidian community
- **Collaboration / influence:** Reviewed early drafts of the Origin v1.9 architecture; gave specific feedback on the Areas layer; shared his own effort-tracking setup
- **Current status:** Active — occasional async exchange on Discord, occasional coffee in Prague

---

## 🧠 Key Insights / Learnings
- Emphasis on "one capture point, zero exceptions" — no secondary inboxes, not even quick notes apps
- His rule: every project note must answer "what is the next physical action?" or it's not ready to move out of inbox
- Observation: most people fail PKM because they optimize for capture, not for retrieval — the vault should be built for the reader, not the writer
