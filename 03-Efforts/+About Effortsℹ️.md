# 📋 Efforts Folder Contract

**What**: Time-bounded projects and goals with clear end states—what you actively drive to completion. Organized by status (Active/Paused/Waiting).

**When**: Reviewed weekly in relation to Areas. Status updated as progress changes. Completed and archived when finished.

**Where**: `03-Efforts/` → `Active/`, `Paused/`, `Waiting/` subfolders by status.

**Lifecycle**: Create via QuickAdd when you commit to goal. Move between folders by status. Archive to 06-Archive when complete.

---

## ✅ What Belongs Here

- Time-bounded projects with goals
- Experiments and challenges with end dates
- Efforts connected to Areas for strategic alignment
- Work driving toward specific outcomes

## ❌ What Doesn't Belong Here

- **Ongoing life domains** → [[Areas]] (no end date)
- **Knowledge distilled from effort** → [[02-Knowledge]] (atomic insights)
- **External sources** → [[04-Sources]] (to be processed)

## 📖 Real Effort Examples

> [!example]+ **Effort: Learn Spanish Basics**
> - **Goal**: Hold 10-minute conversation
> - **Duration**: 3 months  
> - **Phases**: Assessment → Basics → Conversation Practice
> - **Status**: 🔄active
> - **Integration**: Daily notes track practice

> [!example]+ **🏗️ Complex Effort: "Learn Spanish Basics"**
> - **Duration**: 3 months  
> - **Phases**: Assessment → Basics → Conversation Practice
> - **Success**: Hold 10-minute conversation with native speaker
> - **Integration**: Daily notes track practice, Sources hold resources
> - **Template Used**: Extended Effort Template with milestones
> - **Outcome**: Achieved conversational level, continuing advanced

> [!example]+ **🌱 Experimental Effort: "Morning Meditation"**
> - **Duration**: 30-day trial
> - **Hypothesis**: 10min meditation improves focus scores
> - **Measurement**: Daily focus rating 1-10
> - **Decision Point**: Continue/adjust/stop after 30 days
> - **Template Used**: Experimental Effort Template
> - **Outcome**: 23% focus improvement, made permanent habit
